PROJECT SUMMARY

Title:
Beyond Scores: Designing Health Data for Human Understanding

Subtitle:
Using NHANES data to explore user-centered approaches to health measurement

PURPOSE
This project explores ways to represent health data so that meaningful patterns and relationships are easier to see than they would be from a single summary score.

ANALYSIS 1 — DIETARY BEHAVIORAL FINGERPRINTS
NHANES dietary data are used to categorize reported foods into broader food groups and create heatmaps for two contrasting dietary patterns.

The visualizations compare categories including fruit, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein, dairy, processed snacks, processed foods, sugary drinks, added sugar, and water.

The purpose is to show the composition of a behavioral pattern rather than reduce it to one overall score.

ANALYSIS 2 — NETWORK ANALYSIS
The notebook combines behavioral, wellbeing, and biological measures and examines their correlations.

Measures include physical activity, sleep, depression symptoms, self-reported health, smoking, BMI, CRP, caffeine, and alcohol.

The correlations are represented as a network to explore where variables sit relative to the broader system of health-related measures.

DATA
Public NHANES data.

TOOLS
Python, pandas, NumPy, seaborn, Matplotlib, NetworkX.

PORTFOLIO TAKEAWAY
The project focuses on the analytical judgment involved in deciding how health information should be represented. A score can summarize a state, but a pattern can reveal what is changing and where differences occur.
